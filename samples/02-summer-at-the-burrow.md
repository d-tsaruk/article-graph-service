# Summer at the Burrow

The Boy Who Lived, the orphan raised by the Dursleys at Privet Drive, spent the summer at the Burrow with his
best friend Ronald. Mr. Weasley, Ronald's father, who works at the Ministry of Magic and loves Muggle gadgets,
kept asking the boy about electric plugs. Mrs. Weasley, Ronald's mother, cooked enormous dinners for her
husband and children. Percy Weasley, Ronald's older brother and a serious, ambitious student, spent his
evenings writing letters.

Everyone at the Burrow feared Tom Riddle, the young wizard who later took the name Lord Voldemort and
murdered the parents of the Boy Who Lived.
