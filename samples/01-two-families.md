# Two Families

Harry Potter is an orphaned young wizard. His parents, James Potter and Lily Potter, were killed by the dark
wizard Lord Voldemort when Harry was a baby. Harry grew up at number four, Privet Drive, with his aunt
Petunia Dursley and her husband, his uncle Vernon Dursley. Their son Dudley Dursley is Harry's cousin.
For years Harry was bullied by Dudley.

At school Harry became best friends with Ron Weasley. Ron is the youngest son of Arthur Weasley and
Molly Weasley, who have been married for many years. Arthur works at the Ministry of Magic, and Molly runs
their crowded home, the Burrow, where she treats Harry like one of her own children.
